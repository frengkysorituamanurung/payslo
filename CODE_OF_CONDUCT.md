# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
adapted for a small, local training lab. The summary below describes the behavior we expect from
everyone who participates in this repository, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other project space.

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in PaySLO Lab a welcoming and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Our standards

Behavior that strengthens the community includes:

- Using welcoming, inclusive, and professional language.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully and offering it thoughtfully.
- Focusing on what is best for the learners and maintainers of the project.
- Assuming good intent while addressing the technical substance of a disagreement.

Behavior that is unacceptable includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption in any project space.
- Hate speech, discriminatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing private information, such as physical or email addresses, without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional learning
  environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project
in public spaces. Project spaces include the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, review comments,
and any chat or discussion channel operated for the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct,
and will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers through a private
channel or by opening an issue that describes the incident without including sensitive personal
information. Maintainers will review reports promptly and respond proportionally, ranging from a
private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. Reports will be handled with
discretion, and retaliation against anyone who reports an incident in good faith is not tolerated.
